Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Lethal Grid is a free browser game. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or payment details.

Your progress stays in your browser

Your save (gold, stages, upgrades, stars and settings) is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data, or using Reset progress in the game's settings, deletes it.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count anonymous gameplay events, such as stages cleared, upgrades bought and errors, so we can balance and fix the game. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, which it does not store. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google AdSense

Ads are optional rewards in Lethal Grid. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere on the internet. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors at aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Children

Lethal Grid is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
